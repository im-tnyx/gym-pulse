# GymPulse

GymPulse is a gym management, member retention, attendance, renewal, and revenue operations platform.

## Product goal

Build a practical operating loop for gyms:

`attendance -> risk detection -> follow-up -> member return -> renewal -> relevant add-ons -> measurable outcomes`

## Initial scope

- Member and membership management
- QR attendance and assisted check-in
- No-show / churn-risk red list
- Staff follow-ups and next actions
- Membership renewals and payment states
- PT, diet, and product add-ons
- Owner dashboard and daily operational summary
- Notifications, consent, and audit history

## Product direction

GymPulse will start as a focused gym operations MVP and evolve into a multi-tenant SaaS platform with CRM, trainer workflows, workout programming, progress tracking, multi-branch operations, analytics, and AI-assisted insights.
